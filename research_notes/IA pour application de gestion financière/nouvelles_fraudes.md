# New and emerging financial fraud (2024-2026) that a business financial management application must defend against

Research date: 17 November 2026. Every figure carries its year. Method note: most figures below were read from search-result summaries of press or professional coverage of the official reports; the primary PDFs (linked) were located but **not opened**, except two pages fetched in full (La finance pour tous on the OSMP 2025 report; Financial Afrik on WAEMU e-money liability). Vendor sources are tagged **[vendor]**. Software countermeasures are mostly my own design inferences and are placed under "Inferences", not presented as sourced facts.

## 1. Which fraud types are growing fastest in 2024-2026?

### Takeaway
The fastest measured growth is in fraud where the legitimate user is manipulated into paying (APP / "fraude par manipulation", BEC, fake supplier, fake bank adviser), now amplified by generative AI (voice/video deepfakes, AI-written emails, AI-forged documents), and in SIM swap / account takeover. Card fraud is falling while transfer fraud rises: the attack has moved from the payment instrument to the person and to the supplier master data.

### Cited Findings

**Manipulation / APP / transfer fraud**
- France, 2025: fraud "par manipulation" (fake bank adviser, fake bank anti-fraud service, fake president, IBAN substitution) reached EUR 516 million, +34% in one year; it is 41.6% of all payment fraud, roughly double its share four years earlier — [La finance pour tous, on the OSMP 2025 report](https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2026/09/14/moyens-de-paiement-les-fraudes-par-manipulation-en-forte-hausse/)
- France, 2025: transfers became the most-defrauded instrument, 39% of fraud value (30% in 2024); card fraud fell 8.6% to EUR 475 million, card fraud rate 0.0472%, lowest on record — [La finance pour tous](https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2026/09/14/moyens-de-paiement-les-fraudes-par-manipulation-en-forte-hausse/)
- UK, 2025: APP fraud losses +19% to GBP 576.4 million; cases +7% to 248,070; investment scams +40% to GBP 221.5 million — [UK Finance press release, Fraud Report 2026](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/news-and-insight/press-release/fraud-report-2026-press-release); [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/cybersecurity/2026/united-kingdom-app-fraud-jumps-19percent-scammers-abuse-online-platforms)

**BEC / CEO fraud / fake supplier**
- US, 2025: BEC 24,768 complaints and USD 3.046 billion losses, up from 21,442 complaints and USD 2.77 billion in 2024 — [Adams and Reese on the FBI 2025 Internet Crime Report](https://www.adamsandreese.com/the-ledger/fbi-releases-2025-internet-crime-report-key-takeaways-for-the-payments-industry)
- France, 2026 barometer (Allianz Trade with Odoxa, 315 executives): 85% of mid-caps and large companies suffered at least one fraud attempt in the past year; external fraud is 88% of cases, led by fake supplier (42%) and fake client (41%); 76% of executives think AI increases the risk — [Option Finance](https://www.optionfinance.fr/info-financiere-en-continu/d/2026-09-21-hausse-des-fraudes-52-des-grandes-entreprises-et-eti-sous-pression-allianz-trade.html); [Journal de l'économie](https://www.journaldeleconomie.fr/fraudes-entreprise-5-mesures-concretes/)
- Europol IOCTA 2026 (published 28 April 2026, subtitle "How encryption, proxies and AI are expanding cybercrime") reports AI-assisted impersonation of bank staff and law enforcement and increased realism of BEC and CEO fraud — [Europol IOCTA 2026 PDF](https://www.europol.europa.eu/cms/sites/default/files/documents/IOCTA-2026.pdf); [eucrim summary](https://eucrim.eu/news/iocta-2026/)

**Deepfakes, synthetic identities, AI agents**
- US, 2025: the FBI attributed more than USD 30 million of BEC losses to AI-generated emails and voice cloning, and says this is likely an undercount; 2025 is the first year AI-assisted fraud appears as a category in IC3 statistics — [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/ai-assisted-fraud-makes-big-debut-in-fbis-cybercrime-stats-624819?edate=20260408&eid=1)
- **[vendor]** Sumsub Identity Fraud Report 2025-2026 (published 25 Nov 2025, 4+ million fraud attempts from its own customers): "sophisticated" multi-step attacks +180% over 2024-2025 while the overall identity-fraud rate fell from 2.6% (2024) to 2.2% (2025) of verifications; synthetic identity is the top first-party fraud type at 21%; deepfake fraud and money muling 11% each; deepfake attempts in 2025 +96% in France, +94% UK, +84% Spain, +53% Germany; identity fraud +9.3% in Africa, -5.5% in Europe — [Sumsub newsroom](https://sumsub.com/newsroom/sumsubs-annual-report-fraud-shifts-to-complex-multi-step-schemes-in-2025-agentic-ai-scams-poised-to-surge-in-2026/); [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ai-deepfake-fraud-skyrockets/)
- **[vendor]** Sumsub describes emerging "AI fraud agents" that automate full verification flows (synthetic documents, deepfake video, mimicked user behaviour) — [Sumsub newsroom](https://sumsub.com/newsroom/sumsubs-annual-report-fraud-shifts-to-complex-multi-step-schemes-in-2025-agentic-ai-scams-poised-to-surge-in-2026/)
- Europol IOCTA 2026 notes first signs of agentic AI in criminal workflows (systems that plan and execute parts of the criminal process), per a Dutch legal summary — [Bijzonder Strafrecht](https://www.bijzonderstrafrecht.nl/home/iocta-2026-europol-signaleert-groeiende-velocity-gap-tussen-handhaving-en-cybercriminaliteit)
- **[vendor]** Trustpair 2026 report (250 US CFOs/senior finance executives, Dynata, Nov 2025): 71% saw more AI-powered fraud attempts in 12 months; only 32% validate vendor bank details continuously or in real time; 48% still rely on human callbacks or email confirmation — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260126508647/en/AI-Fraud-Outpaces-Human-Defenses-as-71-of-U.S.-Companies-Report-Rise-in-Attacks-Trustpair-Report-Finds)

**AI-generated documents (receipts, invoices)**
- **[vendor]** AppZen data: AI-generated receipts were 70.8% of the fake receipts it flagged by mid-May 2026, from about 0% in March 2025 (1,471 AI fakes, 745 employees, 174 companies). This is the mix among detected fakes, not the fraud rate of all expense claims. Average AI fake about USD 100 vs USD 182 for template fakes: high volume, low value, sized under auto-approval thresholds — [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/use-of-ai-receipts-in-expense-fraud-soars); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3892615)
- **[vendor]** Emburse survey: 34% of US/UK business professionals admit having used AI to generate a fake receipt (self-reported) — [Emburse](https://www.emburse.com/blog/ai-generated-receipts-expense-fraud)
- **[vendor]** SAP Concur: AI receipts are realistic enough that experienced reviewers can no longer rely on visual checks — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-fuels-a-new-wave-of-fake-receipts-according-to-sap-concur/)

**Account takeover, SIM swap, money mules**
- UK, 2025 (Cifas National Fraud Database): record 444,993 filings; account takeover about 78,000 cases (+6%, 18% of filings); telecoms account takeover +105% — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/03/266710-uk-fraud-cases-surge-to-all-time-high-444000-reported-to-national-fraud-database-in-past-year/); [Fintech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/03/16/uk-fraud-hits-record-high-as-ai-accelerates-crime/)
- UK: unauthorised SIM swaps above 106,000 in 2025 (+43% per one source, +38% per another), then +402% in H1 2026 vs H1 2025 (percentage only, no count published) — [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cifas-analysis-shows-uk-needs-better-detection-for-record-identity-fraud); [Zyphe](https://www.zyphe.com/resources/news/cifas-identity-fraud-uk-half-year-2026)
- UK: Cifas reports a 67% increase in the use of **business accounts** by money mules; over 22,000 mule cases filed in 2025 — [Security Matters](https://securitymattersmagazine.com/fraud-cases-hit-record-high-as-criminals-adapt-and-scale-reports-cifas)

**Internal / occupational fraud**
- ACFE 2026 Report to the Nations (14th edition; 2,402 cases investigated Jan 2024-Sep 2025; 143 countries): asset misappropriation in 90% of cases; corruption in 45% (median USD 150,000); financial statement fraud least frequent, median USD 1 million — [ACFE 2026 PDF](https://www.acfe.com/en-us/-/media/files/acfe/pdfs/rttn/2026/2026-report-to-the-nations.pdf); [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/organizations-lose-5-to-fraud-each-year)
- France, 2026: 57% of employees say they have observed at least one fraudulent behaviour in their company (Allianz Trade/Odoxa, 1,005 French respondents) — [Journal de l'économie](https://www.journaldeleconomie.fr/fraudes-entreprise-5-mesures-concretes/)

**Fraud-as-a-service**
- Europol IOCTA 2026: criminal networks increasingly rely on service-based models (malware-as-a-service, phishing kits) exploiting encryption, automation and large volumes of stolen data; online fraud described as the fastest-growing area of organised crime — [FIAU Malta notice](https://fiaumalta.org/news/europol-publishes-the-internet-organised-crime-threat-assessment-iocta-2026/); [dig.watch](https://dig.watch/?p=250999)

### Inferences
- Ranking by evidence of growth (measured, official): (1) manipulation/APP transfer fraud (+34% FR 2025, +19% UK 2025); (2) SIM swap (+38-43% UK 2025, +402% H1 2026); (3) BEC (+10% US 2025); (4) business-account mule use (+67% UK). Ranking by vendor data only: deepfakes (+96% FR), AI-forged receipts, synthetic identities. Agentic AI fraud is still "first signs" (Europol) and prediction (Sumsub), not a measured loss category.
- AI's main measured effect so far is quality and scale of social engineering rather than a separate loss line: the FBI's AI-attributed BEC (USD 30M+) is about 1% of BEC losses (USD 3.05B), and the FBI itself calls it undercounted.
- For a finance application the highest-exposure objects are: the supplier bank-detail record, the payment approval step, the user session/second factor, and uploaded supporting documents.

### Gaps
- No official 2024-2026 loss series isolates fake-invoice fraud generated by AI; only vendor data on receipts and one vendor anecdote on invoices.
- No independent (non-vendor) measurement of deepfake or synthetic identity volumes was found.
- Europol IOCTA 2026 exact wording and figures on fraud-as-a-service were not read in the primary PDF.

## 2. What are the measured losses and trends by official source?

### Takeaway
Reported losses keep reaching records (US USD 20.9 billion in 2025, France EUR 1.24 billion, UK GBP 1.28 billion), but the composition is what changes: card fraud falls, authorised/manipulated transfers rise. Occupational fraud median loss is USD 104,000 per case, with small organisations hit hardest.

### Cited Findings
- **FBI IC3, 2025:** total reported losses USD 20.877 billion, +26% vs 2024; BEC USD 3.046 billion (24,768 complaints), about 14.6% of total; BEC complaint rate roughly stable over three years — [SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/fbi-cybercrime-losses-neared-21-billion-in-2025); [Adams and Reese](https://www.adamsandreese.com/the-ledger/fbi-releases-2025-internet-crime-report-key-takeaways-for-the-payments-industry). Conflict: Paubox cites 22,364 complaints / USD 893 million for BEC, which appears to be the AI-referencing category, not BEC — [Paubox](https://www.paubox.com/blog/us-cybercrime-losses-hit-20.9-billion-in-2025)
- **ACFE 2026:** median loss USD 104,000 per case, mean USD 1,457,000; organisations estimated to lose 5% of revenue to fraud each year; median scheme duration 12 months; schemes lasting over five years median about USD 1.12 million; organisations under 100 employees have the highest median, USD 126,000 — [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/organizations-lose-5-to-fraud-each-year); [Dean Dorton](https://deandorton.com/articles/acfes-2026-report-to-the-nations-highlights-ongoing-fraud-risks-for-organizations/). One summary says the median fell from USD 145,000 in the 2024 edition (not confirmed in the primary source) — [Whispli](https://www.whispli.com/blog/occupational-fraud-2026-acfe-report-to-the-nations-whistleblowing)
- **ACFE 2026, detection and controls:** tips are the first detection method (43% per one summary, "nearly half" per another); management review associated with 55% lower median loss, surprise audits 50% lower; 18 anti-fraud controls studied — [Whispli](https://www.whispli.com/blog/occupational-fraud-2026-acfe-report-to-the-nations-whistleblowing); [Brady Ware](https://bradyware.com/2026-acfe-fraud-report-insights/)
- **UK Finance, 2025 (Fraud Report 2026):** total payment fraud GBP 1.28 billion (+4%); APP GBP 576.4 million (+19%), 248,070 cases; 66% of APP cases originate online, 17% via telecoms; GBP 354.3 million reimbursed (about 61%) — [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/financial-crime-fraud-prevention/uk-payment-fraud-losses-topped-1-28bn-in-2025-new-uk-finance-report-finds); [UK Finance report PDF](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/system/files/2026-06/UK%20Finance%20Fraud%20Report%202026.pdf)
- **Banque de France / OSMP, 2025 (published Sept 2026):** total fraud EUR 1.241 billion (+3.8%) on 7.2 million fraudulent transactions (-7.6%); transfer fraud EUR 484.7 million (+36.7%; EUR 351 million in 2024); transfer fraud is 61% "détournement" (payer manipulated), 27% "faux" (forged order), 3% falsification; instant transfer fraud rate 0.041% (0.046% in 2024); cheque fraud rate 0.069%, the highest of any instrument — [La finance pour tous](https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2026/09/14/moyens-de-paiement-les-fraudes-par-manipulation-en-forte-hausse/); [Meilleurtaux](https://banque.meilleurtaux.com/banque-en-ligne/actualites/2026-octobre/fraude-par-manipulation-516-meur-en-2025-34.html); [OSMP 2025 report PDF](https://www.banque-france.fr/system/files/2026-09/Rapport_2025_de_l_OSMP.pdf)
- **Allianz Trade barometer 2026 (France, with Odoxa):** 85% of mid-caps/large firms targeted; for 19% of them the amount exceeds EUR 10,000 — [Journal de l'économie](https://www.journaldeleconomie.fr/fraudes-entreprise-5-mesures-concretes/). Publication date conflict: 16 June 2026 in some articles, 21 Sept 2026 on Option Finance — [Option Finance](https://www.optionfinance.fr/info-financiere-en-continu/d/2026-09-21-hausse-des-fraudes-52-des-grandes-entreprises-et-eti-sous-pression-allianz-trade.html)
- **Interpol Africa Cyberthreat Assessment:** OCCRP headline on the latest edition: AI involved in 55% of African cybercrimes, losses above USD 480 million; cyber-related crime is over 30% of reported crime in Western and Eastern Africa (2025 edition) — [OCCRP](https://www.occrp.org/en/news/ai-fuels-55-of-african-cybercrimes-as-losses-top-480-million-interpol-warns); [Interpol news release 2025](https://www.interpol.int/News-and-Events/News/2025/New-INTERPOL-report-warns-of-sharp-rise-in-cybercrime-in-Africa)
- **GSMA State of the Industry Report on Mobile Money 2026 (data 2025):** about USD 2 trillion (GSMA page: USD 2.1 tn) in transactions, doubled since 2021; 2.3 billion registered accounts (+268 million); nearly three-quarters of registered accounts inactive monthly, with fraud risk and transaction taxes cited as deterrents — [GSMA report page](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/gsma_resources/the-state-of-the-industry-report-on-mobile-money-2026/); [WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2026/03/30/mobile-money-africa-inactivity-gsma-report-2026/)
- **Europol IOCTA 2026:** qualitative assessment; no loss total found in coverage — [Europol IOCTA 2026 PDF](https://www.europol.europa.eu/cms/sites/default/files/documents/IOCTA-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- French and UK data tell the same story from two regulators: strong customer authentication pushed card fraud down, and fraudsters moved to getting the legitimate user to authorise the transfer. A finance application cannot rely on authentication strength alone; it must check the payee and the context of the order.
- ACFE's finding that small organisations have the highest median loss suggests the target market for an SME finance application is precisely where segregation of duties is weakest, so the software has to supply it.
- All national totals are reported losses; FBI and UK Finance figures are known undercounts (voluntary complaints, member-reported).

### Gaps
- PwC Global Economic Crime Survey, Deloitte, Feedzai, BioCatch, Onfido/Entrust 2025-2026 figures were not searched (call budget); none are cited.
- ACPR, FATF/GAFI and GIABA typology reports for 2025-2026: no dedicated fraud figures retrieved.
- Interpol: scope differs between editions in coverage (43 of 54 member states vs 26 or 36 countries); the 55% AI figure and USD 480M come from a press headline, not the primary PDF.
- GSMA: the claims "90% of mobile money providers experienced identity fraud, 88% social engineering" come from a vendor blog citing GSMA and are unverified — [Youverify, vendor](https://youverify.co/en/blogs/mobile-money-digital-wallet-fraud-africa-detection-prevention)

## 3. What notable real cases exist, and which control failed?

### Takeaway
The reference cases (Arup 2024, Singapore 2025, Ferrari 2024) share one pattern: no system was hacked; a person with payment authority was convinced through a channel treated as proof of identity. Where the attack failed, it was because of an out-of-band human check, not a technical detector.

### Cited Findings
- **Arup, Hong Kong, January 2024:** a finance employee received a message purporting to come from the UK-based CFO about a "secret transaction", joined a video call where the CFO and colleagues were all deepfakes, then made 15 transfers totalling HKD 200 million (about USD 25.6 million). Discovered only when the employee followed up with headquarters. Arup stated none of its internal systems were compromised — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/europe/2024/05/17/arup-deepfake-fraud-scam-victim-hong-kong-25-million-cfo/); [CFO Dive](https://www.cfodive.com/news/scammers-siphon-25m-engineering-firm-arup-deepfake-cfo-ai/716501/)
- Arup, control failure as analysed by secondary/vendor sources (not an official finding): no second approver, no out-of-band confirmation, no system-enforced hold; one employee authorised 15 transfers without independent check — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/security/25-million-deepfake-that-human-review-mfa-and-liveness-detection-all-missed)
- **Singapore multinational, March 2025:** finance director contacted on WhatsApp by a fake CFO, then joined a Zoom call with deepfaked executives including the CEO; asked to transfer about USD 499,000; the scam surfaced when a further USD 1.4 million was requested. Sources conflict on outcome: police-based reporting says "nearly lost"; others say the transfer went out and Singapore's Anti-Scam Centre with Hong Kong police froze most funds — [HCA Mag](https://hcamag.com/asia/specialisation/hr-technology/singapore-firm-nearly-lost-500000-after-deepfake-video-scam-police/531450); [Borneo Bulletin](https://borneobulletin.com.bn/finance-director-nearly-loses-sgd670k-to-scammers-utilising-deepfakes)
- **Ferrari, mid-2024:** an AI-assisted fake-president attempt was narrowly avoided thanks to an executive's vigilance — [Le Monde Informatique](https://www.lemondeinformatique.fr/actualites/lireamp-ferrari-a-evite-de-peu-une-attaque-au-president-par-deepfake-96079.html)
- **France, 2026:** La Banque Postale reports deepfake audio/video impersonations are multiplying but that fake-president and fake-RIB frauds still mostly rely on less innovative approaches; it also relays a DGSI alert on AI and fraud against companies — [La Banque Postale, fraudes au président et au faux RIB](https://www.labanquepostale.fr/entreprises/actualite/2026/fraudes-au-president-et-au-faux-rib.html); [La Banque Postale, IA et fraude](https://www.labanquepostale.fr/entreprises/actualite/2026/ia-et-fraude.html)
- **West Africa:** BEC has driven highly organised multimillion-dollar enterprises such as the transnational syndicate Black Axe; 11 African countries account for most BEC activity originating on the continent (Interpol 2025) — [Interpol news release](https://www.interpol.int/News-and-Events/News/2025/New-INTERPOL-report-warns-of-sharp-rise-in-cybercrime-in-Africa)
- **[vendor]** Ramp reportedly uncovered more than USD 1 million in fake invoices within 90 days — [Beancount blog](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/19/ai-generated-fake-receipts-expense-fraud-guide)

### Inferences
- Failed control, common to Arup and Singapore: identity was verified on the channel chosen by the requester (video call set up by the fraudster). The defence is a verification channel chosen by the company and enforced by the system (callback to a number already on file, approval inside the application by a second authenticated user).
- Splitting into 15 transfers (Arup) and escalating second requests (Singapore) show that per-transaction limits without cumulative limits per beneficiary and per period are ineffective.
- The trigger words are constant: urgency, secrecy, authority. A request flagged "confidential" that bypasses the normal workflow is itself a signal.

### Gaps
- No French company case from 2025-2026 with a published, quantified deepfake loss was found.
- Arup's own post-mortem on controls is not public in the sources found; the control analysis is third-party.
- A circulating claim that "40% of fake-president frauds use deepfakes" comes from an unsourced vendor blog and is not retained; an unsourced "EUR 640 million per year of fake-president fraud in France" likewise.

## 4. For each typology: detectable signals and software controls

### Takeaway
Sourced evidence supports a small set of high-yield controls: payee name/IBAN verification, enforced dual approval, management review and tip channels, and not trusting SMS codes or visual inspection of documents. The rest of the control catalogue below is design inference built on the mechanisms in sections 1-3.

### Cited Findings
- **Verification of Payee (VoP):** the EPC VoP scheme rulebook took effect 5 October 2025; euro-area PSPs must offer it for SEPA credit transfers from 9 October 2025 under the Instant Payments Regulation; non-euro-area PSPs by July 2027 — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/regulations/news/epcs-verification-of-payee-scheme-rulebook-enters-into-effect); [LSEG](https://www.lseg.com/en/insights/risk-intelligence/verification-of-payee-becomes-mandatory-new-regulatory-rules-in-eu-payments)
- UK precedent: a 17% drop in APP fraud losses is cited after Confirmation of Payee (secondary citation by a vendor) — **[vendor]** [Trustly](https://www.trustly.com/blog/new-fraud-prevention-measure-making-payouts-even-more-secure). An "81% reduction in all-cause fraud" in NL/UK is an unverified vendor claim — **[vendor]** [Netcetera](https://www.netcetera.com/stories/news/reducing-fraud-by-verification-of-payee.html)
- **Callbacks are still manual:** 48% of US firms rely on human callbacks or email confirmation to validate vendor bank details; 32% validate continuously (Nov 2025 survey) — **[vendor]** [Business Wire / Trustpair](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260126508647/en/AI-Fraud-Outpaces-Human-Defenses-as-71-of-U.S.-Companies-Report-Rise-in-Attacks-Trustpair-Report-Finds)
- **OSMP advice (2026):** verify a new payee's bank details before adding it, e.g. by "contre-appel"; OSMP asks telecoms to authenticate phone numbers and platforms to cooperate; a single anti-fraud short number (like the UK's 159) is under study — [La finance pour tous](https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2026/09/14/moyens-de-paiement-les-fraudes-par-manipulation-en-forte-hausse/)
- **Occupational fraud controls (ACFE 2026):** management review associated with 55% lower median loss; surprise audits 50%; presence of a reporting mechanism shortens median duration (11 months with, 17 without, per one summary); tips are the top detection source; 84% of fraudsters show at least one behavioural red flag — [Whispli](https://www.whispli.com/blog/occupational-fraud-2026-acfe-report-to-the-nations-whistleblowing); [Brady Ware](https://bradyware.com/2026-acfe-fraud-report-insights/); [Folsom Local News on ACFE](https://lifestyle.folsomlocalnews.com/?p=662352)
- **SMS one-time codes:** Cifas links the SIM swap surge to security codes sent by text that can be intercepted to bypass account protections — [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cifas-analysis-shows-uk-needs-better-detection-for-record-identity-fraud)
- **Document forgery detection:** tools analyse image metadata and inconsistencies (timestamps, server identifiers, travel patterns); visual review alone is no longer reliable — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/workers-are-scamming-their-employers-using-ai-generated-fake-expense-receipts); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-fuels-a-new-wave-of-fake-receipts-according-to-sap-concur/)
- **Liveness is not sufficient alone:** a secondary analysis titles the Arup case as one "that human review, MFA and liveness detection all missed" — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/security/25-million-deepfake-that-human-review-mfa-and-liveness-detection-all-missed)
- **Cross-sector data sharing:** Interpol calls the lack of real-time data sharing between banks, telecoms and police a dangerous blind spot in Africa — [Capmad](https://www.capmad.com/article/interpol-alerte-sur-un-angle-mort-critique-entre-banques-telecoms-et-police-en-afrique)

### Inferences
Design catalogue (signals -> controls). These are my inferences from the mechanisms above, not sourced prescriptions.

| Typology | Detectable signals | Software controls |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier / IBAN change fraud (fake supplier) | Bank-detail change request by email; new IBAN in a different country or bank than history; change shortly before a large invoice is due; sender domain look-alike; first payment to a new account | Treat any bank-detail change as a high-risk event: VoP/name-IBAN check at entry and again at payment; cooling-off period before the new IBAN is payable; mandatory callback to a contact already on file, logged in the app; second approver distinct from the person who edited the record; immutable audit trail of master-data changes |
| CEO fraud / deepfake voice or video | Request outside normal workflow; urgency plus secrecy; new beneficiary; unusual amount, hour, or country; requester asks to move to WhatsApp or a private call | No payment can be created outside the workflow; four-eyes approval enforced by the system (no override by role "CEO"); cumulative limits per beneficiary and per day; hold on first payment to a new beneficiary; in-app step-up confirmation by the named executive via their own authenticated session |
| BEC (mailbox compromise, invoice interception) | Reply-to mismatch; changed bank details in a PDF invoice vs supplier record; invoice number sequence or template differences; mail-forwarding rules | Compare IBAN on every incoming invoice (OCR) to the validated supplier record and block on mismatch; supplier portal for invoice submission instead of email; DMARC/sender checks shown to the user |
| AI-generated fake invoices / receipts | Missing or inconsistent metadata; duplicate or near-duplicate images; amounts just under approval thresholds; supplier not found in company registry; VAT number / SIREN invalid; round amounts; invoice without purchase order or goods receipt | Three-way match (order, receipt, invoice); registry and VAT-number validation; duplicate detection by hash and by content; e-invoicing via certified platforms (structured data from source rather than PDF); statistical outlier tests on thresholds; AI-image forensics as one signal, never the only one |
| APP fraud against the company's own staff (fake bank adviser) | Login followed by adding a beneficiary and an immediate instant transfer; remote-access tool active; session from new device | Delay and second approval for new beneficiaries; device binding; warnings at the point of payment; block payment while screen-sharing is detected |
| Account takeover / SIM swap | New device or location; password reset followed by beneficiary change; phone number recently ported; impossible travel | Phishing-resistant MFA (passkeys / FIDO2) instead of SMS OTP; step-up authentication on sensitive actions; session risk scoring; notify all admins on credential or phone-number changes; delay sensitive actions after a credential reset |
| Synthetic identity / fake client | Identity document without history; reused selfies or device across accounts; company recently registered, no web footprint; mismatched registry data | KYB against official registries; document plus liveness plus device fingerprint in combination; link analysis across clients (shared IBAN, phone, device); credit limits that grow with history |
| Internal / occupational fraud | User creates supplier and pays it; supplier IBAN equals an employee IBAN; payments just under approval limits; manual journal entries at period end; activity outside hours; ghost employees in payroll | Segregation of duties enforced by roles; mandatory review reports for management; continuous monitoring rules (employee-IBAN match, split payments); anonymous tip channel built in; tamper-evident logs; surprise-audit exports |
| Money mules (company or its clients used as conduit) | Rapid in-and-out flows; many unrelated payers; new business account with volume inconsistent with declared activity | Velocity and pass-through rules; beneficiary screening; suspicious-activity reporting workflow |
| Mobile money fraud | Fake deposit SMS; reversal requests after "erroneous" transfer; agent float anomalies; SIM recently swapped; OTP requested by phone | Reconcile against operator API or statement, never against an SMS or screenshot; wait for settlement before releasing goods; SIM-swap check via operator API where available; agent-level anomaly monitoring; never display or ask for PIN/OTP in support flows |
| Fraud-as-a-service / AI agents | Scripted, high-velocity sign-ups; uniform behavioural patterns; headless browsers | Bot detection, rate limits, behavioural biometrics, velocity checks at onboarding |

- Priority order suggested by the loss data: (1) supplier bank-detail verification plus enforced dual approval (covers fake supplier, CEO fraud, BEC, deepfake, which dominate French and US business losses); (2) phishing-resistant authentication (SIM swap growth); (3) document integrity and three-way match; (4) internal-fraud monitoring and tip channel (ACFE).
- VoP checks the name-IBAN match only at the bank at payment time; an application should run the equivalent check earlier, when the supplier record is created or changed, because a "match" on an account opened by a mule in a look-alike company name remains possible.
- Detection of deepfakes themselves is an arms race; process controls that do not depend on recognising a face or voice are the durable defence (inferred from Arup, Singapore, Ferrari).

### Gaps
- No measured results of euro-area VoP after October 2025 (fraud reduction, false "no match" rates) were found; the ABBL (Luxembourg) "results and first feedback" release exists but its figures were not retrieved — [ABBL](https://www.abbl.lu/?p=8530)
- No independent evaluation of behavioural analytics / behavioural biometrics effectiveness (BioCatch, Feedzai) was retrieved.
- France-specific tools (SEPAmail Diamond, FICOBA access, the national fraudulent-IBAN file) and the e-invoicing reform timeline were not researched in this pass; they are mentioned only as design ideas, unsourced.

## 5. What is specific to French-speaking Europe and French-speaking Africa?

### Takeaway
In France the dominant business threat is the manipulated transfer (fake supplier, fake president, fake bank adviser), now 41.6% of payment fraud, with instant transfers and mandatory Verification of Payee changing the terrain. In French-speaking West Africa the financial rail is mobile money (248 million e-money accounts in WAEMU at end-2024), where fraud is mostly social engineering, SIM swap, fake deposits and agent fraud, and where the BCEAO framework makes the issuer liable for its distributors. Recent official fraud statistics for WAEMU and CEMAC are scarce.

### Cited Findings

**France / French-speaking Europe**
- 2025: manipulation fraud EUR 516 million (+34%); transfer fraud EUR 484.7 million (+36.7%); instant transfer usage +78% in number and +118% in value in one year — [La finance pour tous](https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2026/09/14/moyens-de-paiement-les-fraudes-par-manipulation-en-forte-hausse/); [Selectra](https://selectra.info/finance/actualites/marche/virement-instantane-fraude-2025-plafond-appli)

**French-speaking Africa (WAEMU / UEMOA)**
- At 31 Dec 2024 (BCEAO data): 69 e-money issuing initiatives (52 banks, 14 e-money institutions, 2 public treasuries, 1 microfinance institution); e-money accounts 248 million, +18.99% from 209 million in 2023; Côte d'Ivoire became the largest mobile money market of the Union by value in 2024, ahead of Senegal — [Financial Afrik, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/10/07/theme-fraude-au-mobile-money-et-responsabilite-de-lemetteur-de-monnaie-electronique-dans-luemoa/)
- Typologies named for WAEMU mobile money: fake deposits, OTP harvesting, SIM swap, account takeover, fraud by distribution agents — [Financial Afrik](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/10/07/theme-fraude-au-mobile-money-et-responsabilite-de-lemetteur-de-monnaie-electronique-dans-luemoa/)
- Legal frame: BCEAO Instruction n°008-05-2015 (21 May 2015): the issuer stays liable to clients and third parties for operations of its distributors notwithstanding any contrary clause (art. 18); complaints system mandatory (art. 30). Instruction n°001-01-2024 (published 23 Jan 2024, in force 1 May 2025) unifies the payment-service-provider regime and defines strong authentication. Operators typically argue the client disclosed the PIN or validated the operation; exemption turns on gross negligence, burden of proof on the provider (author's legal analysis) — [Financial Afrik](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/10/07/theme-fraude-au-mobile-money-et-responsabilite-de-lemetteur-de-monnaie-electronique-dans-luemoa/)
- The BCEAO launched the interoperable instant payment platform PI-SPI on 30 September 2025; commentary warns that interoperability spreads fraud across borders too — [Financial Afrik, 1 May 2026](https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/05/01/interoperability-of-payments-in-sub-saharan-africa-money-has-no-borders-fraud-neither/)
- User exposure: 43% of mobile money users in Senegal and 28% in Côte d'Ivoire reported at least one fraud attempt in the previous 12 months (CGAP survey, published 2024) — [FinDev Gateway](https://www.findevgateway.org/fr/blog/2024/06/des-enquetes-revelent-une-voie-vers-une-finance-numerique-responsable-dans-luemoa)
- Interpol: mobile money fraud is the most prevalent scam, reported by 97% of responding African countries (latest edition, 2025 data); fake operator-agent scams described in Senegal — [Osiris](https://www.osiris.sn/afrique-de-l-ouest-les-arnaques-en-ligne-foisonnent.html)
- Côte d'Ivoire: mobile money fraud is mostly social engineering, plus phishing and SIM swapping (2023 panel, Deloitte adviser); a fintech proposed mandatory identification of mobile money numbers (2025) — [CIO Mag](https://cio-mag.com/cote-divoire-la-fraude-au-portefeuille-electronique-une-cyber-escroquerie-redoutable); [Orishas Finance](https://orishas-finance.com/actualite/lutte-contre-la-fraude-en-cote-d-ivoire-une-fintech-propose-une-identification-obligatoire-des-numeros-mobile-money-836caf18?lang=en)
- GIABA reported USD 2.1 billion of suspicious cryptocurrency transactions in the region in 2024 (forum in Monrovia, Nov 2025) and classes mobile-money fraud among cybercrime linked to money laundering — [Osiris](https://osiris.sn/blanchiment-de-capitaux-et-le-financement-du-terrorisme-lie%CC%81s-a%CC%80-la.html)
- Outside the French-speaking zone but indicative of SIM swap dynamics: Kenya +327% SIM-swap fraud, about USD 3.8 million, 123,000+ fraudulent SIM cards in 2025 (secondary coverage of Interpol); SIM swap rising in Tanzania and Uganda — [Facephi observatory, vendor](https://facephi.com/observatory/en/484-millones-de-dolares-perdio-africa-por-ciberfraude-en-un-ano-segun-interpol/); [The Citizen](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/cyber-threats-outstrip-digital-growth-in-e-africa-interpol-5212790)
- Old baseline: 41,894 electronic-transaction fraud cases recorded in WAEMU in H1 2019 — [Orishas Finance](https://www.orishas-finance.com/actualite/transactions-electroniques-41-894-cas-de-fraudes-enregistres-dans-l-uemoa-au-premier-semestre-2019-94a6efbb?lang=en)

### Inferences
- A finance application for WAEMU/CEMAC businesses must treat mobile money as a first-class payment rail: reconciliation against operator data (not SMS or screenshots), handling of reversal scams, agent/cashier-level controls, and no reliance on SMS OTP given SIM swap.
- In WAEMU, issuer liability for distributors (art. 18) and the 2024 strong-authentication definition mean dispute evidence matters: the application should keep verifiable logs of who initiated and approved each mobile money operation.
- For France, the product should integrate VoP results into the supplier record and payment workflow, and keep cheque handling (still the highest fraud rate) inside the same controls as transfers.
- PI-SPI instant interoperable payments will probably reproduce the European pattern (instant, irrevocable transfers raise the cost of manipulation fraud), but no WAEMU data yet supports this; it is an assumption.
