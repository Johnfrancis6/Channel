# Storytelling rules for "Who Pays?"

Writing rules for Whose Dime scripts, with numbers a checker can test (S03).
Each rule comes from the niche's hits and flops, from the Zehon audit, or
from both. Thresholds live in [`style_thresholds.json`](style_thresholds.json);
the metrics are computed by [`tools/measure.py`](tools/measure.py). The rule
table below is generated by [`tools/make_rules.py`](tools/make_rules.py):
edit `RULES` there, not the table.

## What was measured (measured)

- **Corpus**: 20 hits (≥ 2× their channel's median views) and 10 flops
  (≤ 0.5×), 8 to 25 min, published 2025-04-22 to 2026-10-23 (window: from 2024-11-07), from Crayon
  Capital, Built On You, CEOzoned and JunkBondInvestor
  ([`corpus/index.csv`](corpus/index.csv)). MagnatesMedia has no video of
  8-25 min in the window (its videos run ~30 min).
- **Contrast**: the Zehon glass and aluminium scripts (French, measured with
  short French word lists; jargon not measured).
- **Per-text metrics**: [`corpus/metrics.csv`](corpus/metrics.csv), 32 rows.
  Check of the tool: Zehon sentence-length SD comes out 4.79 (glass) and 4.15
  (aluminium); the audit measured 4.8 and 4.1 with another script.

## The main finding: nothing blocks

**No metric separates hits from flops**: on all 23 metrics, the hits'
interquartile range overlaps the flops'. By the S02 rule, every rule is
therefore `warn`, none `block`. Style is a warning; facts (S03, D4) are
what block.

What does hold is **direction inside each channel** (4 channels have both
hits and flops). In all 4, hits:

1. name a person earlier (S1);
2. use fewer numbers (S7);
3. say "you" more (S11);
4. repeat their favourite sentence opener ("And", "But") *more*, not less
   (S12): the Zehon rule "≤ 3 'Sauf que'" does not carry over to English.

A 4-out-of-4 sign has a 1 in 8 chance under no effect (two-sided sign
test): a hint, not a proof. Those four rules get the tighter hits' quartile
as threshold; the others get the hits' 10th or 90th percentile, or a number
set by the plan or the report (named in the source column).

**How noisy are the warnings?** Applying all 14 rules to the corpus
(`make_rules.py`): a hit trips a median of **3** rules, a flop **4**, each
Zehon script **3** (glass: S1, S3, S4; aluminium: S1, S4, S7). Most hit
alerts are S14 (16 of 16 hits with jargon: the niche does not explain
terms with marker words) and S3 (captions rarely mark quotations). A script
written to these rules, with quotation marks, avoids both.

## By ear (Franco's 20 s listening, D6)

Rules no metric here can test. Franco checks them on the 20 s sample and on
the script read aloud.

| Id | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **A person to follow.** In the cold open we can picture one named person at one moment: a place, a gesture, a line. | Crayon report (hits: "Bayside, Queens, 1962"); Zehon audit S3 |
| E2 | **The lines sound spoken.** Every quoted line is short and could be said by a person in a room, not read from a filing. | Crayon report (mini-scenes); Zehon audit S6 |
| E3 | **Money is clear the first time.** Every amount says who pays it and to whom; no sentence needs to be heard twice. | Whose Dime promise ("follow the dollar"); critique type `comprehension` |
| E4 | **The narrator reacts.** At least once per beat the narrator is surprised, amused or blunt ("Yes, really."). | Zehon audit S5 (tone fixed to "calm"); Crayon hits |
| E5 | **Each beat ends on a pull, not a formula.** The last line of a beat makes us want the next one, with no stock connector. | Zehon audit S1 ("Sauf que" tic); PLAN_ACTION beat 4 (mini-suspense) |

## Limits (what is assumed)

- **Small n** (20 hits, 10 flops; one channel has a single flop). Medians
  move with one video. Topic, thumbnail and timing drive views more than
  wording; this corpus cannot separate them.
- **Transcripts are captions**, not scripts: punctuation is automatic,
  quotation marks are rare (S3 is a lower bound in the niche), sponsor
  reads are included.
- **Views are lifetime totals**: older videos are favoured. Videos younger
  than 14 days were excluded; Built On You and CEOzoned are a month old.
- **The person detector is a list method** (given names, titles, then
  surname reuse). It misses single names without a title (Pliny, Tiberius)
  and catches a few false ones ("King Kong"). S03 should pass the people
  registry (`people=` in `measure()`).
- **The jargon "explained" test is crude**: marker words only. It found
  almost no explanations in the niche, which may be the method as much as
  the niche.
