## Description

For years, an Uber ride cost you less than it cost to make. One analyst, Hubert Horan, estimated that passengers paid only 41 percent of what their trips cost.[^F05] So who paid the rest? We follow the dollar from a snowy night in Paris to the first full year of operating profit, in 2023:[^F24] the investors, the drivers, and the taxi owners of New York.
